# traveler-1800

One sentence explaining the modern internet to a traveler from 1800, for the
make.fun competition "One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800". The field is
full of libraries, post offices and roads with letters arriving before the
ink dries — this entry takes a different picture instead: distance itself
has collapsed, so every house on earth now stands on your street, and a
knock on any door is answered before your hand leaves the wood. No word in
it was coined after the traveler's time.

> Every house on earth stands on your street now, so you may knock on any door and be answered before your hand leaves the wood.

The program in `main.py` checks the line against the competition rules (a
single sentence, 1–200 characters, ending with a period) and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`.
